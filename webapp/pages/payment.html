🎫 Generate Voucher Range

Creates one blank row per voucher number. Every payment entry (from any type, or Excel upload) must use a voucher number that already exists here — this is what lets you spot a missed voucher (a blank row) and stops the same voucher number being reused for two different parties.

Prefix is optional. e.g. Prefix D, 1 → 500 creates D1…D500 (doctor payments). Leave prefix blank for plain numbers.
